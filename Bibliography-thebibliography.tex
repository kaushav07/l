\section{Introduction}
My field of interest is Mathematics. While studying, I have used many books. For basic real analysis, the book by Bartle and Sherbert \cite{bartle} is very useful. For algebra, students often read Herstein \cite{herstein} and Gallian \cite{gallian}.

\section{Other Books}
For linear algebra, the book by Hoffman and Kunze \cite{hoffman} explains vector spaces clearly. A good book for calculus and analysis is by Rudin \cite{rudin}, though it is a bit tough for beginners.

Many references can be cited together, for example \cite{bartle, rudin} are both books on analysis.
